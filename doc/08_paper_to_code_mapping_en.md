# 08 Paper-to-Code Mapping

This is the central paper-to-repository map. The status labels are:

- `fully implemented`: the paper mechanism has a concrete executable counterpart.
- `partially implemented`: the idea exists, but some substructure is simplified or missing.
- `approximated in code`: the paper concept is represented by a higher-level software approximation.
- `represented mainly by scripts/configs`: the paper idea appears mostly as experiment configuration rather than reusable logic.
- `only used in evaluation`: mostly downstream reporting.
- `not clearly found`: the paper names a mechanism that does not appear as a clear code artifact.

## Strongest Correspondence

- SEC attention-guided pruning in `doc/paper/tex/5.semantic.tex` maps cleanly to `algorithm/focus/main.py` and patched decoder logic in `algorithm/focus/models/qwen2/modeling_qwen2.py`.
- The algorithm-to-simulator bridge claimed in `doc/paper/tex/7.evaluation.tex` maps cleanly to `algorithm/focus/main.py`, `simulator/models/sparse_info.py`, and `simulator/main.py`.
- DSE in `doc/paper/tex/7.evaluation.tex` maps cleanly to `algorithm/run_dse.sh`, `simulator/run_dse_sim.sh`, and `evaluation_scripts/plot_scripts/ipynb_src/figure_10.ipynb`.

## Weakest Correspondence

- The paper’s SEC hardware sub-blocks such as the top-k bubble sorter and offset encoder are not exposed as equally explicit software or RTL modules.
- The paper’s layouter and scatter blocks are conceptually strong, but their checked-in implementation evidence is distributed across simulator code and block-level RTL fragments rather than a single integrated artifact.
- The DRAMsim3 claim in `doc/paper/tex/7.evaluation.tex` is weaker than the checked-in runtime path in `simulator/arch/accelerator.py`.
